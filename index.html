Bury your pension in the garden

Explore the use of gold coins in the UK as a drawdown retirement strategy.

This site was motivated by belief that we are probably entering a period of financial repression due to very large fiscal deficits.

With rising tax rates and changing pension rules the question arises "What is the cost of completely opting out of the pension system?"

That is, what is the cost of cashing-in your whole pension and holding it as gold coins and then periodically selling to fund retirement.

This is obviously not very tax efficient as you will almost certainly be paying higher rate tax on a large portion of it and then there are the transaction cost of changing gold back into money when you need to but despite all of that it appears that there are certain time periods in the past when it would have been a good strategy.

By default, this tool adjusts yearly withdrawals for UK Inflation (CPI) to maintain constant purchasing power throughout your retirement.

This is NOT financial advice.
